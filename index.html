About Me

I believe the strongest brands don't shout louder—they understand people better.

Hi, I'm Sumedha Hegde. My journey into AI copywriting started with curiosity—not just about technology, but about people. Before writing a single sentence, I ask questions. What is someone quietly feeling? What are they hoping for? What problem are they trying to solve? Those answers shape every headline, every product description, and every brand story I create.

AI helps me work faster, but empathy, strategy, and human insight guide every creative decision. Whether I'm building a wellness journal, a coffee brand, or a SaaS product, my goal remains the same: Create experiences that feel honest, thoughtful, and deeply human.
